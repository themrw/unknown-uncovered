Arithmetic (ours, from the tonnages above; gold density 19.32 t/m³):
- 224,200 t → 11,605 m³ → cube side 22.6 m (~0.54 × India Gate)
- 3,672 t / (22.64 m)² = 0.37 m new layer per year
- 54,770 t → 2,835 m³ → cube side 14.2 m
- 22.64 m − 20.12 m = 2.52 m ("करीब ढाई मीटर" longer than the pitch)

Note: WGC's goldhub "how much gold" page shows reserves ~61,000 t (Q2 2026 basis); we use and attribute the Metals Focus end-2025 figure (54,770 t).
